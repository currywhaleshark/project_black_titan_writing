## Locked Gate A constraints

### S6

- Treat the two contacts as one problem, not two unrelated monster battles.
- Insert one or two daily-life chapters between retreat and return.
- Improvements may enhance search accuracy, fixation, or transport.
- Do not make the returning individual a Black-Titan-killing countermeasure.

### S7

- Use at most two simultaneous individuals.
- Keep each individual inside noncombat retrieval functions.
- Difficulty comes from role coordination, not swarm numbers or combat design.
- Sua gives one short, decisive observation about the two roles.
- Sua does not navigate continuously, replace Dana's senses, or prove piloting
  ability in spring. Dana executes the win and bears the risk.
- Neither individual recognizes Black Titan as kin. Its spring role is the
  mobile obstruction that cancels first-lineage searching.

### Dana's symptoms

Spring permits only deniable signs:

- momentary focus blur
- fatigue or brief illness after battle
- short hand numbness
- prescription change

Do not use visual-field loss during battle, inability to stand after sortie,
objective collapse, or an undeniable diagnosis before summer.

### Hye-jeong

Her spring line is:

`hurt → anxiety → concern → restraint`

- She notices that Dana is absent or unwell around attacks.
- Her core question is closer to “Are you sick or in danger?” than “Why are
  you lying?”
- She chooses to remain near Dana rather than force an answer.
- Move house surveillance, military-equipment evidence, tailing, Myeong-jun
  consultation, and military checkpoint pressure to summer.
- Summer pursuit must grow from protective concern, not curiosity, jealousy,
  or a desire to expose the secret.

- Hye-jeong's concern, not evidence
