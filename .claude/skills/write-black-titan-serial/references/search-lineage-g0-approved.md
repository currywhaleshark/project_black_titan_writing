## Canon mechanism

1. When the first giant was destroyed, the incubator acquired enough of its
   neural and field signature to construct a lineage-specific search wave.
2. The incubator assumes a first-lineage giant survives somewhere in
   Cheonghae. It does not know that the actual responding source is Dana in
   human form.
3. Dana derives from the first-giant lineage. Outside Black Titan, a first-
   lineage search wave can resonate with her.
4. Sua and Black Titan derive from the second-giant lineage. They do not
   resonate with the first-lineage search wave.
5. When Dana is inside Black Titan, Titan's second-lineage field cancels the
   first-lineage search wave around her. Search-wave symptoms must not occur
   inside the cockpit.
6. Any onboard symptom must instead arise from cross-lineage piloting,
   connection strain, or another separately established cause.
7. The incubator and its spring units do not recognize Black Titan as kin or
   as the retrieval target. They record it as a mobile surface obstruction and
   cancellation source that appears when the first-lineage response vanishes.

## Spring applications

### S1

- The lobster type emits the strong active first-lineage search wave.
- Dana resonates before boarding; Sua does not.
- After Dana boards, the response vanishes and Black Titan appears. The
  incubator records correlation, not kinship.

### S2

- The sea-urchin type passively listens for residual first-lineage response.
- Access denial reduces vibration and signal interference around the listening
  point.
- It does not emit the strong active pulse, so Dana's C1 symptoms do not
  advance.
- Black Titan's arrival is renewed interference, not target confirmation.

### S3

- The spider-crab type uses narrow directional first-lineage scans from high,
  electromagnetically clear structures.
- Dana may show one brief response before boarding when a scan crosses her.
- Once she boards, scan-related symptoms stop.
- When Black Titan enters its cancellation radius, the monster abandons that
  perch and seeks a higher or more distant clean scan point.
- The winning method may use Black Titan as a mobile jammer and herding wall.
  Do not use the discarded logic in which the monster stays above Titan,
  attaches to its shoulders, or measures Titan at close range.
- Dana's later prescription change remains a separate, deniable C1 signal
  associated with VR and repeated connection, not cockpit exposure to the
  search wave.

### S4–S7

- Binding and transport units may restrain or relocate Black Titan so
  first-lineage searching can continue.
- They may recover the true first-lineage target if it is found.
- They do not bind Black Titan because they recognize it as kin.
- Spring remains inside search and retrieval protocols. Elimination
  reclassification does not become effective until summer.
