## Spider-crab mission lock

1. After the S2 fixed passive search fails, the spider-crab type searches from
   high electromagnetic structures outside the cancellation radius.
2. Its eight long legs act as separated directional sensing supports while it
   intermittently scans for first-lineage response.
3. Dana may resonate briefly outside Black Titan. Sua and Black Titan remain
   nonreactive because they are second-lineage.
4. Once Dana boards, Black Titan's second-lineage field cancels the search wave
   around her.
5. The individual does not identify Black Titan as kin or the retrieval target.
   It only detects a dirtier search environment and moves to a higher, farther
   position.
6. Safety blackouts follow from field interference and occupied infrastructure,
   not an intentional attack on the power economy.
