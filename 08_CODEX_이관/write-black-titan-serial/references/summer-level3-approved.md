## What changes in summer

Spring was RETRIEVAL: the creatures came to take the machine away. Summer is
the turn to DESTRUCTION — the enemy reclassifies Black Titan from something to
recover into something to break. Consequences that follow from that one
change:

- Combat-type creatures appear; the gaps between them shorten
- Ranged attack enters the story (spring was all grappling)
- Repeated combat deepens the coupling, so pain starts reaching the pilot
- Bare hands stop being enough, and the ANCHOR is developed and fitted
- Sua begins substitute sorties when Dana cannot fly
