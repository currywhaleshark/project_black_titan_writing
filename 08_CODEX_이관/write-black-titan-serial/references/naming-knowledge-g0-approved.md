# Approved naming knowledge asymmetry

## Authority

- Status: `APPROVED_G0`
- Request: `G0-003`
- Approved by the user: 2026-07-30
- Scope: every outline, brief, draft, report, dialogue, internal monologue,
  close viewpoint, and knowledge ledger until an explicit in-story name reveal

This rule overrides older outlines and style examples that let outsiders use
`블랙 타이탄` or `타이탄`.

## Internal proper name

`블랙 타이탄` and `타이탄` are currently internal names known by:

- Dana
- Sua
- Young-jin

Do not add another holder because they are family, friendly, observant, or in
the military. A future holder must explicitly receive or discover the name in
approved story events.

Author-facing planning, neutral metadata, and family-close narration may use
the internal name. This does not grant that knowledge to an outside character.

## Outside-character rule

Until an explicit reveal, outsiders may not use the internal name in:

- spoken dialogue
- internal monologue
- text messages, posts, slogans, or school writing
- military reports, identifiers, or orders
- a knowledge-table cell attributed to that outsider
- narration tightly limited to that outsider's lexicon

This includes Seong-ho, Hye-jeong, Ye-jin, Ju-ho, school characters, residents,
and the current military.

## Allowed lexicons

### School and public

Use what could arise from observation:

- `검은 거인`
- `검은 로봇`
- `검은 수호자`
- `그 거인`
- `우리 동네 거인` after the Gurae Port victory

Do not force the whole city to use one official name. Different speakers may
prefer different informal labels.

### Military

Use temporary identifiers and behavior classifications:

- `미식별 검은 거인`
- `미식별 거대 개체`
- `소속 불명 거대 개체`
- `소속 불명 전략무력`

Example:

`미식별 검은 거인 — 소속 불명, 현재 방어행동 반복`

### Seong-ho

Preserve his fast, specification-heavy, exaggerating voice. He may invent:

- technique names
- rankings
- an adult left-handed ace profile
- fan nicknames for the black giant

He may not accidentally invent the exact internal name `블랙 타이탄`, and he
must not shorten it to `타이탄`.

## Narration rule

- Family-close POV: internal name allowed.
- Outsider-close POV: use that person's public or military lexicon.
- Neutral narration: internal name allowed only when it cannot be mistaken for
  the outside character's knowledge.
- Author-facing outline mechanics: internal name allowed.
- Knowledge and disclosure tables: vocabulary inside a holder's `knows`,
  `suspects`, or `records` cell must match that holder's lexicon.

## Gate D check

Search every draft for `블랙 타이탄` and `타이탄`.

For each hit, identify:

1. speaker or thought-holder
2. viewpoint holder
3. document or record owner
4. whether the line is neutral author narration

If any holder is outside the current name whitelist, replace the term with an
allowed observed label. The lexical checker reports these names as a knowledge
warning; the warning is not an automatic failure because family and
author-facing uses remain valid.
