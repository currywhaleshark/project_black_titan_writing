## Near-loss and solution

- The near-loss comes from the binding topology, not low output or nerve
  damage.
- Dana first pulls arms apart one by one. Freed arms rebind other joints while
  separately anchored tow arms divide her posture.
- Dana recovers by stopping the backward struggle and rushing into the current
  pull during an anchor-transfer gap.
- Southern Defense Command supplies only evacuation, empty-pier, and collapse
  radius information.
- Dana deliberately accepts a shallow rebind, changes angle between empty
  piers, crosses the tow lines, moves into the pull, flips the body onto the
  dry slope, and disables the central locomotor organ with body weight and
  unarmed grappling.
- Do not add a cable, chain, Iron Anchor, fixed weapon, military finishing
  strike, or Sua's tactical navigation.

- Seong-ho remains at the rear of the evacuation line to pass a frightened
  younger student toward a teacher.

  younger child inward and steps badly.
- The result is an ankle sprain or ligament injury that puts him in a cast for
  school, not a severe fracture or life-threatening injury.
