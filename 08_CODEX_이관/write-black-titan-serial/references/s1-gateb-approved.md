## Locked knowledge and cover story

### Family cover

Young-jin tells Dana and Sua:

1. Black Titan was a pre-Deluge classified military research institute
   prototype for ultra-large disaster response.
2. It was designed for rubble removal, flood defense, and heavy rescue, then
   discarded during the prototype demonstration stage. It has no fixed weapons
   because it was never intended as a combat platform.
3. The Deluge and war severed the responsible organization and records, so the
   current Southern Defense Command does not know it exists.
4. Young-jin participated in the project and removed the prototype in defiance
   of a disposal order.
5. The VR session five months earlier calibrated the controls to Dana's
   response data, making her the only immediately usable first pilot.
6. Exposure would lead to seizure of the Titan, Young-jin's arrest, and the
   sisters' separation under protective custody.

This is an approved in-story lie, not Black Titan's canonical origin. The real
origin remains Hyun-seo's alien-cell removal and giant cultivation followed by
Young-jin's concealment.

Treat disaster response as the sole claimed purpose and the design reason for
the absence of fixed weapons. Do not add another intended use. The user
explicitly selected this formulation on 2026-07-30.

### Knowledge asymmetry

- Dana knows the Titan, her sortie, the VR-calibration cover, and the claimed
  theft consequences. She does not know the biological origin, neural lineage,
  signal leak, or Young-jin's responsibility for targeting Cheonghae.
- Sua learns the Titan and Dana's first sortie in EP2. She does not know her own
  compatibility, neural lineage, or future pilot role.
- The current Southern Defense Command confirms only that the giant lacks a
  current identifier or equipment-list match. This does not authenticate
  Young-jin's account of the old project.
- The public knows only that an unidentified black giant saved the city.
- Hye-jeong knows Dana lied and looked unwell. Her emotional line is concern,
  not accusation or evidence gathering.
- `블랙 타이탄` and `타이탄` are family-internal proper names currently known
  by Dana, Sua, and Young-jin. Seong-ho, the school, the public, and the
  military do not know them.
- Public characters use observed labels such as `검은 거인`; the military
  uses temporary identifiers such as `미식별 검은 거인` or
  `소속 불명 전략무력`.
